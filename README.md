## 🧠 About Me

I didn't set out to become a developer. I started by trying to automate one boring task with a Python Telegram bot. Out of pure curiosity, that single script snowballed into self-hosted AI assistants, full-stack web apps, security tools, and open-source templates.

I'm a student who learns by building in public and breaking things until they make sense. Rather than just using high-level tools, I like digging into how things actually work underneath, from hosting local LLMs with Ollama and wiring them to WhatsApp or Telegram, to understanding network traffic and cybersecurity.

My philosophy is simple:
> **`Build → Break → Understand → Improve → Repeat.`**

- 🌐 Building and maintaining **[naborajs.me](https://naborajs.me)** as my personal web ecosystem and digital lab.
- 🎮 Creating gaming content and sharpening video production through **NS GAMMING**.
- 🤝 **Open to Collaborate:** Always happy to connect with people who enjoy **building real things instead of just talking about them**. Open an issue, start a discussion, or reach out at **[msg@naborajs.me](mailto:msg@naborajs.me)**.
